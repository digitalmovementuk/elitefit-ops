      Good questions

      Do I need a membership?

      No. Book a single class (300K) or bring a friend on the Duo Day Pass (225K each). Members book in the GymMaster app or at the front desk. You can also join through ClassPass.

      I'm a total beginner. Where do I start?

      Start with Boxercise Beginner, Booty and Core or Off-Court Training. All three welcome beginners.

      How long is a class?

      An hour, whichever class you pick.

      What if I can't make it?

      Please cancel in advance so someone else can take your spot. If nobody has arrived 15 minutes after the start, the class is called off.

      Is there an age limit?

      Boxercise is recommended from age 14.

      Are weekend classes every week?

      Not every week. The weekend times in the schedule are the usual pattern, so ask us on WhatsApp which dates are on.
