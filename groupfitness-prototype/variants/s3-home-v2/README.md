## Direction in three lines

1. s3-home, the warmest Elite Fit page, kept as it is: white cards on warm club footage, the swinging name tags, the dealt photo deck, the terracotta +1, one terracotta button per screen.
2. A class-schedule booking page: a live Now / Next bar, a hero with the Book, the Google rating and a real quote on the first screen, then the week at a glance, then the next classes with their own footage.
3. Booking in two short steps, one decision each: who and how (the coach, their invitation, a real review in full, four big tiles), then your details (name, WhatsApp, pay now or at reception, Confirm). A generic Book (hero, sticky bar) first asks how you want to pick: By day, By class, Show week, or the next class in one tap (the shared engine-v2 class finder, in this page's style).

## Production data note

The prototype sends nothing: `EF.api.ENDPOINT = null` and `EF.api.submit()` resolves locally after 600 ms. `EF.api.payload(booking)` builds the record a live page would POST as JSON to one first-party endpoint (for example a same-origin WordPress REST route that forwards to **HubSpot** or the **club's booking system**, GymMaster): `schema_version`, `booking_ref` (idempotency key), `created_at`, `intent` (class_booking | membership_start | waitlist), `class_id`, `class_name`, `occurrence_id`, `class_start/end` (+08:00), `coach_id/name`, `pass_id` (class-1 | duo | perform-4w | elite-4w | member), `people`, `unit_price_idr`, `total_idr`, `currency`, `membership_start_date`, `pay_choice`, `payment_status`, `payment_method` (null), `payment_ref`, `full_name`, `first_name`, `whatsapp_e164`, `whatsapp_country_code`, `is_member`, `returning_device`, `waitlist`, `reminder_channel` ("whatsapp"), `marketing_opt_in` (false), `privacy_notice_version`, `source_page`, `source_section`, `utm_*`, `referrer_origin`, `locale`. **No email anywhere.** HubSpot: upsert the contact on `whatsapp_e164`; the booking as a deal or custom object.
