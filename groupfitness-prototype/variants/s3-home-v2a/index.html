    Which fitness classes in Canggu suit you?

    Our fitness classes in Canggu come in four kinds: boxing, conditioning, sculpt, and mobility and stretch. Each of these gym classes lasts an hour.

    Looking for a boxing class in Canggu?

    Boxercise is boxing training with no physical contact, built on strength, speed, agility and endurance. If you're new, start with Beginner, then move up to Advanced. Rey teaches both, six classes a week. It's recommended from age 14.

    Quick answers

    What do people ask first?

    How much does a day at the gym in Canggu cost?

    A day here is 225K each on the Duo day pass, for two people: gym floor, every class and Recharge. On your own, a class drop-in is 300K, without the gym floor.

    How much is a gym membership in Bali?

    At Elite Fit, 4 weeks costs 2.2M for Perform (gym and classes) or 2.7M for Elite (adds Recharge). Weekly memberships start at 1.1M, and longer ones are on our full price list.

    Which class is best for beginners?

    Three of our classes welcome beginners: Boxercise Beginner, Booty and Core, and Off-Court Training. Tell your coach it's your first class when you arrive, and see the first-class plan above.

    Is there a HYROX gym in Bali?

    We run HYROX Conditioning, a class inspired by the HYROX race format, three times a week with Rey: Monday and Tuesday at 8am, Thursday at 8:30am.

    Do I need a membership to join a class?

    No. Book a class drop-in for 300K, or the Duo day pass for two, on this page. Members book in the GymMaster app or at the front desk, and you can also join through ClassPass.

    Can I pay at reception?

    Yes. When you book, pick “Pay at reception” or “Pay now”, whichever suits you. Either way, you give your full name and WhatsApp so we can hold your spot.

    What if I can't make it?

    Please cancel in advance so someone else can take your place. If nobody has arrived within 15 minutes of the start time, the class is cancelled.
